# Livelatch Terms Of Service Draft

This draft describes expected service terms for the Livelatch MVP. It is product documentation, not legal advice, and should be reviewed before publication.

## Service Overview

Livelatch provides tools for creators to manage a public profile, links, blocks, themes, LatchDeck experiences, and related account settings. LatchID provides the branded identity layer used across Livelatch and related products.

## Accounts

Users are responsible for keeping account access secure and for making sure the information they provide is accurate. Users should not share credentials or use another person's account without permission.

Livelatch may restrict or suspend accounts that appear to be abusive, fraudulent, harmful, unlawful, or in breach of these terms.

## User Content

Users are responsible for the content they add to Livelatch, including profile text, links, images, blocks, themes, and any connected social profiles.

Users should not upload or link to content that is illegal, misleading, harmful, infringing, abusive, or designed to compromise the platform or other users.

## Social Connections

Future LatchID social connections may allow users to connect external platforms. Users remain responsible for complying with the terms of those external platforms.

Disconnecting a social account should stop new syncing where supported, but some historical operational records may remain for security, billing, audit, or troubleshooting purposes.

## Subscriptions And Billing

Some Livelatch features may be free, paid, usage-based, or limited by subscription plan. Billing providers may process payment details and invoices on behalf of Livelatch.

Plan details, prices, trials, renewals, cancellations, and refunds should be clearly shown in the billing experience before paid launch.

## Availability

Livelatch may change, pause, or remove features as the product evolves. The service may occasionally be unavailable because of maintenance, incidents, infrastructure providers, or third-party integrations.

## Platform Safety

Users must not attempt to:

- Attack, scrape, overload, or reverse engineer the service.
- Bypass authentication, authorization, rate limits, billing, or security controls.
- Upload malware or harmful code.
- Misrepresent identity or impersonate another person.
- Use Livelatch for spam, phishing, scams, or unlawful activity.

## Intellectual Property

Livelatch owns the platform, brand, product design, and code unless otherwise stated. Users keep rights to their own submitted content, but grant Livelatch the permission needed to host, display, process, and distribute that content through the service.

## Open Source And AGPL-3.0 Compliance

Livelatch is based on [LinkStack](https://linkstack.org/), which is distributed under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.en.html). The AGPL-3.0 includes source-code availability obligations for network-accessible modified versions.

Livelatch should provide clear access to the corresponding source code for the deployed fork, including Livelatch-specific modifications where required by the license. The My Data page includes a placeholder `View source` action that should eventually link to the appropriate public GitHub repository or source distribution page.

Users should understand that open-source license obligations apply to the software platform code, while user-submitted profile content remains governed by the user-content terms above.

## Termination

Users may request account closure. Livelatch may suspend or terminate accounts that breach terms, create risk, or require action for legal, safety, billing, or operational reasons.

## Disclaimers

The service is provided as available. Livelatch should not promise uninterrupted access, error-free operation, or fitness for every use case unless a future paid agreement explicitly says otherwise.

## Open Work

Before publication, Livelatch should confirm the governing law, billing terms, refund rules, acceptable-use detail, support contact, dispute process, AGPL-3.0 source availability process, GitHub/source URL, and any consumer-law requirements for the launch regions.
