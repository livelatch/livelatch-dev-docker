# Livelatch Privacy Notice

This draft explains how Livelatch expects to collect and use data for the current MVP. It is product documentation, not legal advice, and should be reviewed before being published as a formal privacy policy.

## What Livelatch Collects

Livelatch may collect account, profile, billing, and usage data so the service can create and maintain creator profiles, links, LatchDeck experiences, subscriptions, and support workflows.

Typical data categories include:

- Account details such as name, email address, username, role, and authentication identifiers.
- LatchID identity data needed to connect a person to Livelatch and related services.
- Public profile content such as profile names, descriptions, links, blocks, images, and theme choices.
- Social connection metadata when a user chooses to connect a social platform.
- Billing and subscription references needed to manage plans, invoices, and access.
- Operational logs, security events, and basic analytics used to keep the platform reliable.
- Support, feedback, and communication records when a user contacts Livelatch.

## How Livelatch Uses Data

Livelatch uses data to:

- Provide and secure user accounts.
- Render public creator profiles and link blocks.
- Save theme, dashboard, and profile preferences.
- Manage LatchDeck cards, redemptions, and future creator tools.
- Process subscriptions and billing through configured billing providers.
- Send service notifications and account messages.
- Detect misuse, investigate incidents, and maintain platform reliability.
- Improve product workflows and understand feature usage.

## LatchID And Supabase

LatchID is the branded identity layer for Livelatch. Supabase provides the backing authentication, database, and integration platform for parts of the LatchID experience.

Some identity, social connection, notification, or future cross-product data may be stored or synchronized through Supabase-backed systems so Livelatch, LatchDeck, and related products can share a consistent account identity.

## Sharing And Processors

Livelatch may share data with infrastructure and service providers that help operate the product, such as hosting, database, authentication, media storage, billing, analytics, email, and support systems. These providers should only receive the data needed to perform their service.

Livelatch should not sell personal data. If this changes, the privacy notice and consent model must be updated before launch.

## Retention

Livelatch should keep data only as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal or financial obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and support legitimate operational needs.

Some records, such as invoices, security logs, and backups, may need to be kept for longer than active profile data.

## User Rights

Depending on location, users may have rights to:

- Request access to data held about them.
- Ask for corrections.
- Request deletion.
- Request a portable copy of their data.
- Object to or restrict certain processing.
- Withdraw consent where processing depends on consent.

These rights may be subject to verification, fraud prevention, legal retention, billing, and security requirements.

## GDPR Notes

For users in the UK or European Economic Area, Livelatch should be prepared to document:

- The lawful basis for each category of processing.
- Data processor relationships.
- International transfer safeguards where relevant.
- Data retention periods.
- How users can contact Livelatch about privacy requests.

## Open Work

Before this text is used publicly, Livelatch should confirm the actual production processors, retention rules, analytics tools, support mailbox, data export logic, deletion logic, and legal contact details.
